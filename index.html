Our story

Margaret and Sam Holloway opened The Floret in 2014 in what used to be a flower shop on Linden Street. The name stayed, and so did the wide front window.

We are a small team of six readers. We don’t stock everything, on purpose. Every title is chosen by someone who has read it and would happily press it into your hands. If we don’t have what you’re looking for, we’ll order it in, usually within three days.

Most of all, we wanted a shop where nobody minds if you stay for an hour and leave with nothing. Most people leave with something anyway.

— Margaret, Sam & the team
